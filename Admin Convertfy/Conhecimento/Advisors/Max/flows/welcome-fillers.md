# O que é

Os emails educacionais entre o welcome email #1 e o last chance de
[[welcome-flow]]. Ele reconhece que o nome é ruim: "filler gives it a bad kind of
like word where it's like not necessary. But these are essentially educational
emails" (L1612-1616).

Quantos: **1-5** na fala (L1612, L2178) e no corpo do slide (L3547); **1-4** no
título do mesmo slide (L3544). O critério é o produto: "if you need a lot of
education for your product, then send some more emails. If your product's a
little bit more straightforward, then maybe you only need like one or two"
(L1618-1622).

Regra dura: todo filler lembra o desconto. "You can plug in anything with
relevant information as long as you remind of the discount in every single one
of these emails" (L1630-1634, L3519).

# Por que incluir

Slide, verbatim (L3551-3554):

> ✅ **Build Trust** – Most customers won't buy immediately. These emails bridge the gap.
> ✅ **Overcome Objections** – Answer unspoken questions that stop people from converting.
> ✅ **Position Your Brand** – Show what makes you different, how your product works, and why it's worth the price.
> ✅ **Prime Future Buyers** – Even if they don't buy now, you've laid the groundwork for later.

# Os 7 fillers que ele julga na aula

Estes sete ele demonstra com exemplos de marca e comenta (L1818-2130). Os
templates de seção são dele; os exemplos foram lidos em voz alta e a
transcrição é ASR — fidelidade menor que a de um artefato de slide.

| Filler | Objetivo | Template de seções | Linha |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Our story** | "build trust by making the brand feel human" — founder, origin story, mission | hero com foto do founder · bridge com a história · product section com links sutis | L1818-1856 |
| **FAQ** | "overcome objections that are silently killing your convergence" (ASR de *conversions*); o cliente não pergunta, não responde email, não aciona o suporte | hero declarando o FAQ · bridge explicando · product section com a resposta da marca | L1858-1934 |
| **How it works** | educar sobre o funcionamento, reduzir confusão | hero (ele prefere before/after ou o processo) · bridge explicando · product section | L1936-1986 |
| **What's inside** | ingredientes ou materiais; "transparency builds trust" | benefício no topo, "what's inside" fechando | L1988-2016 |
| **Social proof** | "reinforce trust by showing proof from your customers" | variações: celebridades/influencers, testemunhos de cliente, bloco de reviews | L2018-2036 |
| **How we're different / us vs them** | "why buy from us rather than buy from one of the other thousands of other brands in our niche" | declaração direta do diferencial, depois seção us vs them | L2038-2082 |
| **Bestsellers** | destacar o produto principal, ou vários, se houver muitos SKUs | destaque de produto único ou grade de bestsellers | L2084-2130 |

**Onde ele expressa preferência:** só uma vez, e é para o Our story — "our story
email is great. I love to include these." (L1854-1856). Sobre o FAQ ele dá uma
regra de dosagem: "you can do one FAQ or you can do like multiple different
FAQs" (L1934). O corpus **não dá ordem de execução** nem critério para escolher
qual filler usar em qual marca.

# Os 21 ângulos do slide

Verbatim, L3558-3578. Lista de opções, não de recomendações — nenhum é
comentado ou classificado:

> * **One Benefit Feature:** Pick one benefit from your product and make a whole email just talking about that one thing.
> * **One Feature:** Pick one feature from your product such as it's ingredients or materials and make a whole email just talking about that one thing.
> * **How It's Made:** Go through the process of how your item is made, it's interesting and builds trust with the customer.
> * **How To Use:** Show the customer how to get the most out of your product with instructions and tips.
> * **Trending:** Highlight products that are selling well in the season or month you are in.
> * **Testimonials:** Highlight 1-5 reviews that are on your site to boost credibility.
> * **Us vs Them:** Compare your product and how it compares to other options on the market.
> * **Myths vs Facts:** Overcome certain beliefs in your niche and position your product as a solution.
> * **Blog Promo:** Highlight a blog that is on your site and drive traffic to it.
> * **Research Study Highlight:** Find a research study online that is related to your product and it's ingredients / material to prove that it is effective.
> * **Customer Transformation**: Highlight real stories of how your product or service has positively impacted or transformed a customer's life or business.
> * **Before vs After**: Showcase the effectiveness of your product or service through compelling before-and-after comparisons.
> * **Note From The Founder**: Provide a personal message from the founder or CEO, sharing insights, company news, or a personal touchpoint with subscribers.
> * **Gift Guide**: Curate a list of recommended products or services for various occasions, helping subscribers find the perfect gift.
> * **Treat Yourself**: Encourage self-care and indulgence with products or services designed to pamper or treat the subscriber.
> * **Media Publications**: Compile mentions of your company in the media, highlighting press coverage, interviews, or articles.
> * **Behind The Scenes**: Offer a glimpse into the inner workings of your company, showcasing the people, processes, or stories behind your products.
> * **Tips and Tricks**: Provide valuable advice, hacks, or strategies to help subscribers get more out of your products or improve their lives in some way.
> * **UGC Content**: Feature user-generated content, such as customer photos, reviews, or stories, to build community and authenticity.
> * **Staff Picks**: Highlight favorite products, books, or tools selected by team members, offering a personalized recommendation to subscribers.
> * **Brand Values**: Communicate the core values and mission of your brand, reinforcing your commitment to certain principles and building trust with your audience.

São 21 itens. A versão falada dessa mesma lista (L2136-2166) traz 14 e é
**subconjunto estrito** da do slide: faltam sete — Research Study Highlight,
Media Publications, Behind The Scenes, Tips and Tricks, UGC Content, Staff Picks
e Brand Values — e nenhum item é exclusivo da fala ("even more testimonials",
L2148, é o `Testimonials` do slide, L3563). Ele fecha a lista dizendo que é
"stuff that we've used in our welcome flow" (L2170).

# Onde o corpus discorda

- **1-5 contra 1-4**, no mesmo slide: heading L3544 diz "Insert 1-4 Filler
  Emails", o corpo L3547 diz "the 1–5 educational emails". A fala diz 1-5 duas
  vezes (L1612, L2178).
- **21 contra 14 ângulos**, entre slide (L3558-3578) e fala (L2136-2166).

# O que o corpus não diz

- Nenhum critério para escolher **qual** filler usar. Existe catálogo, não
  existe ordem nem árvore de decisão.
- Nenhuma ordem recomendada entre os fillers dentro da sequência.
- Nenhum exemplo visual sobreviveu. Os exemplos de marca existem só como
  descrição falada; o slide do welcome não traz nem slots "**Email Example:**",
  que os outros flows trazem.
