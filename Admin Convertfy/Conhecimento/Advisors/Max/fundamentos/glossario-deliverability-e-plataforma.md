### 🧼 **List Health & Deliverability**

* **List Cleaning** – Removing cold subscribers who haven’t engaged in 60–90+ days.  
* **Suppression List** – List of contacts who should never be emailed again.  
* **Warmup (or Rewarm)** – Process of gradually rebuilding sender reputation when deliverability is low.  
* **Spam Trap** – Fake emails used by inbox providers to catch spammers.  
* **Smart Sending** – Klaviyo feature that skips sending to people recently emailed. **Turn off for flows**\!  
* **Domain Reputation** – Score based on how inbox providers trust your email-sending behavior.  
* **Hard Bounce** – Email cannot be delivered permanently (invalid address).  
* **Soft Bounce** – Temporary failure (inbox full, server issue).  
* **Sender Reputation** – Inbox providers’ trust score for your domain.  
* **DKIM / SPF / DMARC** – Email authentication protocols.  
* **Re-engagement Campaign** – Emails trying to win back cold subscribers.  
* **Inbox Placement** – Where the email lands: inbox, promotions, spam.

### 🛠 **Platform & Design Terms**

* **Klaviyo** – The most used eCommerce email platform, especially for Shopify.  
* **ESP (Email Service Provider)** – Software used to send marketing emails (e.g., Klaviyo, Mailchimp).  
* **Subject Line** – Main headline in the inbox.  
* **Preview Text** – Secondary text that appears next to subject line in inbox.  
* **Call-to-Action (CTA)** – The link or button that encourages the reader to take action.  
* **A/B Testing** – Testing two versions of an email (subject, design, copy, etc) to see which performs better.  
* **Dynamic Blocks** – Personalized content shown based on data (e.g., gender, location, product viewed).  
* **Conditional Split** – A rule in a flow that changes the email path depending on subscriber behavior or attributes.  
* **Alt Text** – Descriptive text shown when an image fails to load.  
* **Responsive Design** – Optimized for both desktop and mobile.  
* **Mobile-First Layout** – Designed to perform best on mobile devices.  
* **Email Width** – Standard width is 600–700px to ensure compatibility.  
* **Modular Design** – Repeatable blocks you can mix and match.
