### 🎯 **Segmentation & Targeting**

* **Segment** – A dynamic group of contacts who meet certain criteria (e.g. opened an email in the last 30 days).  
* **List** – A static group of contacts, typically from a specific source (e.g. quiz opt-in, lead gen).  
* **Engaged Segment** – A group of subscribers who have opened or clicked emails recently (14/30/60/90 day).  
* **Exclusion Segment** – A segment you exclude from campaigns (e.g. unengaged, buyers, VIPs).  
* **Tag** – Metadata attached to subscribers (e.g. gender, quiz results, etc.)  
* **Dynamic Segment** – Automatically updates as people meet criteria.  
* **Static Segment** – Manually added users, does not update automatically.  
* **VIP Segment** – High spenders, repeat buyers, or top 1% customers.  
* **Non-Buyers** – Subscribers who have never purchased.  
* **Multi-Buyers** – Customers who’ve purchased 2+ times.  
* **Segment Conditions** – Rules that define who enters a segment.  
* **Personalization Tokens** – Dynamic fields like {{ first\_name }} that personalize the email.

### 📈 **Performance Metrics**

* **Open Rate** – % of people who opened your email. Target: 45%+ for engaged segments.  
* **Click Rate (CTR)** – % of total recipients who clicked. Target: 2–4%+.  
* **Click-to-Open Rate (CTOR)** – % of openers who clicked. Measures email effectiveness.  
* **Conversion Rate** – % of recipients who completed a purchase.  
* **Revenue per Recipient (RPR)** – Revenue divided by total recipients.  
* **EPC (Earnings Per Click)** – Revenue earned per click.  
* **Unsubscribe Rate** – % of people who unsubscribed. Target: \<0.2%.  
* **Bounce Rate** – % of emails not delivered. Target: \<2%.  
* **Spam Complaint Rate** – % of users who mark your email as spam. Target: \<0.1%.  
* **Deliverability** – Likelihood that your emails land in the inbox (vs. spam or promotions tab).
