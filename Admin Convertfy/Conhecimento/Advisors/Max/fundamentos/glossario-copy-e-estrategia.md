# Copy, testes, analytics, compliance e estratégia

### 🧠 **Copywriting & Content**

* **Direct Response Copy** – Copy meant to drive immediate action.  
* **Long-Form Email** – Detailed storytelling email (usually \>250 words).  
* **Skimmable Copy** – Designed for fast, mobile-first reading.  
* **Bridge Section** – Visual/graphic transition that keeps readers scrolling.  
* **Bullet Block** – List of short benefits or features.  
* **Hero Section** – Top of email, usually contains headline \+ main CTA.  
* **Microcopy** – Tiny bits of text like CTA button copy or disclaimers.  
* **P.S. Section** – Postscript, often used for urgency, testimonials, or CTAs.

### 🧪 **Testing & Optimization**

* **Multivariate Testing** – Testing multiple variables (e.g. subject \+ design).  
* **Heatmap** – Shows where users click or scroll in your emails.  
* **Clickmap** – Visual tool to track which links/buttons get the most clicks.  
* **Time Delay** – Wait time between automated flow emails.

### 📊 **Analytics & Attribution**

* **UTM Parameters** – URL tags for tracking in Google Analytics.  
* **Klaviyo Revenue Attribution** – Revenue tracked back to an email send.  
* **Last Click Attribution** – Revenue attributed to last click before purchase.  
* **Open Attribution** – Assigns value to emails that were opened before purchase.  
* **Custom Metrics** – Track specific actions (e.g., quiz completions).  
* **Predictive Analytics** – Klaviyo feature for estimating next order date, churn risk, etc.

### 🔐 **Compliance & Consent**

* **Opt-In** – When a user gives permission to receive emails.  
* **Double Opt-In** – User confirms their subscription via a second email.  
* **GDPR** – EU law regulating data and email marketing consent.  
* **CAN-SPAM** – U.S. law requiring unsubscribe links and contact info.  
* **CASL** – Canada’s anti-spam law.  
* **Unsubscribe Link** – Required link to opt out of future emails.  
* **Preference Center** – Page where users can choose what types of emails they want to receive.

### 💡 **Strategy & Frameworks**

* **Value Stacking** – Showing multiple benefits to increase perceived product value.  
* **Offer Ladder** – Moving from low-friction opt-in to higher ticket offers.  
* **Customer Journey** – Mapping email content to each stage (awareness, consideration, purchase, retention).  
* **Trust Threshold** – The moment a customer finally trusts you enough to purchase.  
* **Margin Preservation** – Making sales *without* using discounts (via value-based or content-driven emails).  
* **List Monetization** – Strategies to convert subscribers into customers over time.  
* **The 80/20 Rule** – Focus on the flows or campaigns that drive the majority of revenue.  
* **Content Buckets** – Themes you can rotate weekly (e.g. education, social proof, product drop, lifestyle).  
* **Email Calendar** – A scheduled map of campaigns by week/month.
