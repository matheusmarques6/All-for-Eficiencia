# A regra

"Your offer is your most important aspect of your pop-up form" (slide L1216;
fala L617). Um form feio com oferta forte ganha de um form perfeito com oferta
fraca: "If you had a terrible unoptimized form, but it was 90% off... people are
going to sign up because it's a really it's a banger offer" (L619).

O piso é ter oferta. "All the brands that grow, they give some sort of offer on
the first purchase" (L613). Se você não quer criar um desconto extra só para o
email, esconda atrás do email o desconto que já dá: "If you give 20% off all
orders uh for your first purchase, then just put that behind like the email"
(L613).

# O teste de sanidade

> **if I was given this offer would I exchange my email for it?**

Verbatim do slide (L1218); na fala, "a great test to ask yourself is if I was
giving this offer would exchange my email for it" (L619-621). O critério de
escolha é margem contra opt-in rate: "Try different offers based off your
margins and opt-in rates" (L1217, L619).

# A regra do AOV $100

| AOV | Formato da oferta |
|---|---|
| **abaixo de $100** | % OFF (L1222, L627) |
| **acima de $100** | $ OFF (L1223, L629) |

O racional é duplo. Percepção: "What do you think sounds better? 10% off or $20
off? If you ask me, Hey Max, do you want 10% off? I'd be like, maybe. But if you
went up to me and said, Hey Max, $20 off, here's a $20 bill. I'd be like, fuck
yeah" (L631). E margem: num AOV de $500, 10% custa $50 em média, e num pedido de
$1.000 custa $100 — com valor fixo "it's just the flat $50" (L1021-1022). Ele
sugere ainda travar mínimo de compra ("you have to spend at least $500 with us
to claim this discount", L1022).

Na direção inversa vale o mesmo teste: com AOV de $50, "$5 bill versus 10% off?
Typically 10% off performs better" (L1023).

# Framing ganha de desconto: o case Gains in Bulk

Exemplo que ele abre ao vivo (buy.gainsinbulk.com, L621). Creatina avulsa a $39
serve só de âncora — "Barely anybody buys this, they are trying to push people
to the subscription" (L623). A assinatura sai $29 (−$10) e vem com free shaker,
free funnel, um **$20 physical gift card** e 90 day money back guarantee (L625).
Conclusão dele: "that's a no brainer offer, that's a banger, it's all about
framing" (L627). Marcas de suplemento, reposição e assinatura têm mais espaço
para isso (L627).

# Free gift: valor percebido alto, custo baixo

O padrão que ele mais elogia. "You've got a free $29 gift" — e o gift é um
porta-joias de viagem que "probably cost them $2 to $3 to produce" (L1024-1025,
L633-635). "So just by changing their offer and framing it as something that is
super valuable, people are going to respond at a high rate and they're going to
be able to keep their margins" (L1026).

# Desconto misterioso

Bônus do slide: "Use a 'Mystery Discount' in your sign-up and give them one of
the above options" (L1226). O racional é a curiosidade: 10% ou 5% off "it's not
very enticing", mas "if you say like, hey get a mystery discount and we will send
it to you over email, a lot of times you get higher options and you go like, oh,
what's that?" (L635-637).

# A lista completa (slide, L1221-1230)

**Main Options** — % OFF (AOV < $100); $ OFF (AOV > $100); Free Shipping;
Free X Gift w/ Order; BONUS: Mystery Discount.
**Other Options** — Giveaway Entry; Free Guide; Early Access.

Sobre o segundo grupo, a fala é explícita: "they don't perform as well, but those
are other options" (L637-639), e giveaway entry em particular "don't perform as
well" (L637). O que ele descarta de saída é oferta que não é oferta: "it's not
exclusive updates or early access. Nobody cares about that because in 2026,
that's literally the bare minimum" (L1018).

# Onde o corpus discorda

Na versão do YouTube a lista curta é "percent off, dollar off, mystery discount,
or free gift" (L1019) — sem free shipping, e com o mystery discount promovido de
bônus a opção principal. Ver `list-growth-lista-de-ofertas`.

# O que o corpus não diz

Não existe percentual padrão recomendado. Os 10%, 15% e 20% aparecem só como
exemplos de demonstração (L613, L661, L728). O único limite superior declarado é
negativo: "as long as you're not giving up like 80% off" (L1019).
