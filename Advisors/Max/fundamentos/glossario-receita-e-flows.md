### 💰 **Revenue Attribution & Metrics**

* **Email Revenue Attribution** – The percentage of your total store revenue that comes from email. Target is \~40%.  
* **Campaign Revenue** – Revenue generated from manual one-off emails.  
* **Flow Revenue** – Revenue from automated emails triggered by specific user behavior (abandoned cart, welcome, etc).  
* **LTV (Lifetime Value)** – The total revenue a customer will generate during their relationship with your brand.  
* **AOV (Average Order Value)** – Average spend per order. Often increased with upsells in flows or bundles in campaigns.  
* **ROI (Return on Investment)** – Email typically delivers \~40x ROI when done right.

### 📬 **Email Types**

* **Campaign** – A one-time broadcast email sent to a list or segment (e.g. promo, product launch, newsletter).  
* **Flow (Automation)** – A series of emails triggered by a customer action (e.g. checkout abandonment).  
* **Transactional Email** – Order confirmation, shipping notification, etc. Often not considered "marketing" but can be optimized.  
* **Text-Based Email** – Email with little to no images or design. Often converts better and improves deliverability.

### 🔄 **Core Automated Flows (Klaviyo-Specific but universal)**

* **Welcome Flow** – Triggered when someone joins your list. Purpose: Convert and onboard.  
* **Site Abandonment Flow** – Triggered when someone visits but doesn't click on products.  
* **Browse Abandonment Flow** – Triggered when a product is viewed but not added to cart.  
* **Cart Abandonment Flow** – Triggered when someone adds to cart but doesn’t check out.  
* **Checkout Abandonment Flow** – Triggered when checkout is started but not completed.  
* **Post-Purchase Flow** – Triggered after purchase. Builds loyalty, reduces remorse, and upsells.  
* **Winback Flow** – Triggered when a customer hasn’t purchased in a while. Goal: Re-engage and convert again.  
* **Sunset Flow** – Triggered when a contact is no longer engaging. Removes or suppresses inactive users.
